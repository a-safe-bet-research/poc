# Data access statement

A Safe Bet: proof-of-concept evaluation

## Summary

The study analyses pseudonymised behavioural data from 5,808 participants on the SunVegas platform (Playtech, United Kingdom), collected through routine platform operation between 1 April 2024 and 31 March 2025 and shared with Erasmus University Rotterdam under the programme's data-sharing agreements. The analysis code and all documentation are open. The underlying data are not in this repository and are held on Erasmus University Rotterdam research infrastructure under the agreements. A pseudonymised participant-level dataset of the aggregated analysis variables is available under controlled access.

## Categories

### Open
Analysis code (`code/`), the analysis preregistration and design documents (`protocol/`), the data dictionary and the data lifecycle and provenance record ([`common/data-documentation/`](https://github.com/a-safe-bet-research/common)), and the tables and figures in `outputs/`. Also deposited at https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/X6BFD. Licences: PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 (code), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (everything else).

### Derived or aggregated only
None at present. The synthetic development dataset generated by `03_poc_simulate.R` is not deposited or available for sharing, because it encodes marginal distributions and transition structures fitted to operator data collected under the data-sharing agreements. Whether sharing a fitted analogue is permitted has not been determined. The research team retains it for audit and verification.

### Controlled access
A pseudonymised participant-level dataset of the aggregated participant-period and participant-month variables used in the analyses. Identifiers are one-way salted; financial values are rounded to the nearest pound; extreme values are top-coded; the dataset satisfies k-anonymity with k = 11. Available to qualified researchers on reasonable request under a data use agreement, subject to approval by the participating operator and the Erasmus University Rotterdam data protection officer. Requests to gamblingresearch@essb.eur.nl. The data management plan names DANS as the intended controlled-access repository; the deposit route will be confirmed following the programme data review, and this statement updated.

### Not shareable
Individual event-level data; the raw operator extracts (Parquet); and the internal rename crosswalk that maps operator schema names to the project's neutral names. Held by the research team on Erasmus University Rotterdam research infrastructure under the data-sharing agreements and not released.

## Variable definitions

`common/data-documentation/` holds the data dictionary and the data lifecycle and provenance record. Cohort definitions, eligibility rules and thresholds are stated in the analysis preregistration in `protocol/`.
