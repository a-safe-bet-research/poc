# Data access statement

A Safe Bet: proof-of-concept evaluation

## Summary

The study analyses pseudonymised behavioural data from 5,808 participants on SunVegas (an online casino licensed in Great Britain, owned by Playtech Plc), collected through routine platform operation between 1 April 2024 and 31 March 2025 and shared with Erasmus University Rotterdam under the collaboration agreement. The analysis code and all documentation are open. The underlying data are not in this repository and are held on Erasmus University Rotterdam research infrastructure under the agreement. A pseudonymised participant-level dataset of the aggregated analysis variables is available under controlled access.

## Categories

### Open
Analysis code (`code/`), the analysis preregistration and design documents (`protocol/`), the data dictionary and the data lifecycle and provenance record (`protocol/03_data_documentation/`; the data dictionary is a copy of the file in [`common/data-documentation/`](https://github.com/a-safe-bet-research/common)), and the supporting information in `outputs/supplementary_material/`. Registered versions at https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/X6BFD. Licences: PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 (code), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (everything else).

### Derived or aggregated only
None at present. The synthetic development dataset generated by `03_poc_simulate.R` is not deposited or available for sharing, because it encodes marginal distributions and transition structures fitted to operator data collected under the collaboration agreement. Whether sharing a fitted analogue is permitted has not been determined. The research team retains it for audit and verification.

### Controlled access
A pseudonymised participant-level dataset of the aggregated participant-period and participant-month variables used in the analyses. Identifiers are one-way salted; financial values are rounded to the nearest pound; extreme values are top-coded; the dataset satisfies k-anonymity with k = 11. Available to qualified researchers on justified request under a data sharing agreement with Erasmus University Rotterdam. Requests to gamblingresearch@essb.eur.nl.

### Not shareable
Individual event-level data; the raw operator extracts (Parquet); and the internal rename crosswalk that maps operator schema names to the project's neutral names. Held by the research team on Erasmus University Rotterdam research infrastructure under the collaboration agreement and not released.

## Variable definitions

`protocol/03_data_documentation/` holds the data dictionary and the data lifecycle and provenance record. Cohort definitions, eligibility rules and thresholds are stated in the analysis preregistration in `protocol/`.
